Prototype. Seeded from published tables and one independent replication. A contributed row needs the split and its size, the exact model, the protocol, and ideally per-instance outcomes so results can be compared pairwise rather than as bare proportions.

Attribution. Every system named here is its authors’ published work. Rows tagged replicated are our re-runs of that work under a stated protocol, not new systems; the ablation strips components from AlphaOPT as published.

Replicated rows use n_runs=1; intervals are wide and no single margin here is statistically significant. Scope is natural language to LP/MILP; constraint-programming benchmarks such as Text2Zinc are tracked separately.
