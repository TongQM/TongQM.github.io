A few selected technical projects. More on [GitHub](https://github.com/TongQM).

## Face classification and verification &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Apr 2025*

Built deep face-recognition models on **VGGFace2** (9K+ identities, 18K+ verification pairs). Trained a ResNet backbone with **ArcFace** loss and contrastive learning to learn discriminative embeddings, reaching **96.7% accuracy** and **3.3% EER** on verification — competitive at-scale identity matching. PyTorch on AWS.

## LoRA fine-tuning for sentiment analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Mar 2025*

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of BERT for five-class sentiment classification on **SST-5**. Inserted **LoRA** adapters over the attention layers and matched full fine-tuning quality with **~10× fewer trainable parameters**, faster convergence, and reduced overfitting. PyTorch on AWS.

## Transformer for PJM regulation-signal forecasting &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Apr 2022*

Sequence-to-sequence Transformer for short-horizon energy-grid signal prediction, achieving **&lt;3% error** on 500-step forecasts and a **25% improvement in compute efficiency** over a comparable LSTM baseline — supporting real-time decisions in grid operations.
